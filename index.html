About Me

A developer who enjoys the space between logic and experience.

I'm Ndoma-Egba Nleyiosowo, a Software Engineering student at Nile University of Nigeria. I enjoy building practical tools, polished interfaces, and systems that feel simple for the people using them.

My work blends corporate structure and creative freedom. As an intern at the Nigerian Communications Commission, I worked with database queries and UI/UX improvements. As a freelance designer, I have created book covers and digital assets for authors including Terrific1 on Inkitt.

Whether I am designing in Figma or developing a full-stack application, I care about clarity, usability, and the small details that make a product feel complete.
